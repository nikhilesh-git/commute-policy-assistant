# Nimbus Tech Employee Transport Policy

Version 1. Owner: Admin and Facilities. Applies to all Nimbus Tech offices in Bengaluru and Pune.

## 1. Purpose and scope

### 1.1 Purpose

This policy explains who can use company-provided transport, how trips are booked, the safety rules for night travel, and the penalties for misuse. It applies to all permanent employees and to contract staff on a Nimbus Tech roster.

## 2. Eligibility

### 2.1 Who is eligible

Employees in grades L1 to L6 are eligible for roster-based pickup and drop. Employees in grade L7 and above receive a monthly conveyance allowance instead and are not eligible for roster cabs, except for drops covered by section 4.1.

### 2.2 Distance rule

Roster transport is provided only when the employee's registered home address is more than 3 km from the office by road. Employees living 3 km or closer may use the shuttle service from the office to the nearest metro station.

## 3. Shift timings and booking

### 3.1 Transport windows

Pickups are provided for shifts that start between 5:00 AM and 11:00 AM. Drops are provided for shifts that end between 6:00 PM and 2:00 AM. Shifts outside these windows require an ad-hoc trip under section 6.1.

### 3.2 Booking cut-off

Roster requests and changes must be submitted in the transport app at least 4 hours before the shift starts (for a pickup) or ends (for a drop). Requests after the cut-off are treated as ad-hoc trips.

### 3.3 Cancellation

A booked trip can be cancelled without penalty up to 2 hours before the scheduled pickup time. Later cancellations count as a no-show under section 8.2.

## 4. Night-shift safety

### 4.1 Escort for women employees

A security escort (guard) is mandatory on every drop of a woman employee after 8:00 PM and on every pickup before 6:00 AM. The cab will not start without the escort on board. This applies to all grades, including L7 and above.

### 4.2 Drop sequence

On cabs carrying women employees after 8:00 PM, a woman employee is never the first person picked up or the last person dropped. The transport desk plans routes to follow this rule.

## 5. Weekends and holidays

### 5.1 Weekend transport

Saturday pickups and drops are available only when the employee's reporting manager approves the request in the transport app by 5:00 PM on Friday. No transport is provided on Sundays.

### 5.2 Public holidays

On national public holidays, transport is provided only for employees on a shift that HR has declared business-critical, and only with HR approval.

## 6. Ad-hoc and special trips

### 6.1 Ad-hoc trips

An ad-hoc trip is any trip outside the roster or booked after the cut-off. Ad-hoc trips must be approved by the employee's reporting manager in the transport app before the cab is dispatched.

### 6.2 Airport trips

Airport pickups and drops are provided only for official business travel. They must be approved by the Travel Desk, and a sedan is assigned. Personal airport trips are not covered.

### 6.3 Cost caps

A single ad-hoc trip may cost at most Rs 1,500. Trips expected to exceed this cap require written approval from the department head before booking.

## 7. Guest travel

Visitors and interview candidates may use company transport when their Nimbus Tech host raises a request at least 24 hours in advance. The cost is charged to the host's department.

## 8. Waiting time and no-shows

### 8.1 Waiting time

The driver waits 5 minutes at the pickup point after the scheduled time. After 5 minutes the cab leaves and the trip is marked as a no-show.

### 8.2 No-show penalties

The first no-show in a calendar month results in a warning email. A second no-show in the same calendar month suspends the employee's roster transport for 7 days.

## 9. Grievances and contact

Complaints about drivers, vehicles or routing are raised with the transport desk through the transport app or at extension 4040. The desk responds within one working day.
